# Words this guide uses

Each term below is the one name for its idea everywhere in the extension, in labels, help text, prompts shown to you and this guide. SillyTavern's own words keep their SillyTavern meaning.

## What it does

### SillyTavern's words

- **Swipe**, **Regenerate**, **Continue** and **Impersonate** are SillyTavern's reply actions. Press and hold on Impersonate shows "Write my next message".
- **Persona** is you in the story.
- **World Info book** is a book of World Info entries; a **World Info entry** is one row of it, with its **Primary Keywords**, **Optional Filter**, **Constant** or **Normal** strategy, **Trigger %** and **Sticky**.
- **Connection Profile** is a saved connection in SillyTavern.
- **First message** is the character's greeting at the start of a chat.
- **Exclude from prompts** keeps a message in the chat and out of what the model reads.
- **Restore default prompt** puts a prompt's shipped wording back.

### The extension's own words

- **Quick action** and **Quick action bar**: a saved tap, and the bar above the message box that holds the pinned ones.
- **Options**: choices for your next move, written by 1 model call.
- **Direction**: words that shape the next reply.
- **Model call**: one request to a model.
- **Story World**: the lasting records of a chat. **Recall**: the records and entries chosen for one reply. **Story state**: where things stand at a reply.
- **Who they are**: a character's portrayal at a reply.
- **Action check**: a die roll for an attempt.
- **Continuity guidance**: guidance that keeps scenes and characters consistent.
- **Follow-through check**: a check that a reply did what it was told.
- **Reply screening**: local checks of a reply's writing.
- **Plot offer**: something the world offers the story.
- **Roast**: a reader's comment on a reply.
- **Reply tray**: the cards about the newest reply, above the message box.
- **Feature**: one part of the extension you can turn on or off. **Ours** and **Theirs** say whether the extension or the selected preset supplies it.
- **Generate sheet**: the sheet a tap opens before a call.
- **Story style**: the look of the story cards in a chat.
- **Scene header**: the time and place line at the top of a reply.
- **Loadout**: a preset saved together with its stack, so you can switch back to both at once.
- **Tracker World Info book**: the book that holds a chat's tracker values.

## Model calls and cost

Nothing in this entry makes a model call.

## Why it works this way

Each idea has one name, so a label, a help line and this guide never call the same thing two ways.
